## 1. الإيميل

**Subject:** Access request: NPM breaking changes dataset (Zenodo 13885487) for a senior project

Dear Dr. Kong,

We are a team of senior students at [University name], [Department], working on our graduation project under the supervision of [Supervisor name, title].

Our project is a tool that detects conflicts between code versions (breaking changes) and between code and its documentation. Your TOSEM 2025 paper, "Towards Better Comprehension of Breaking Changes in the NPM Ecosystem", directly shaped our design. In particular, we follow your distinction between syntactic and behavioral breaking changes, and we are building an export analyzer based on your JavaScript/TypeScript taxonomy.

We would like to evaluate our tool against your annotated data, mainly the RQ2–4 set of 131 projects with breaking changes labeled by category, signature type, and behavior type. We noticed that the replication package on Zenodo (record 13885487) is restricted, and we would be very grateful if you could grant us access.

We will use the data only for academic evaluation within this project. We will not redistribute it, and we will cite your paper and the dataset in our report and any resulting publication.

If it helps, we are happy to share our results with you once the evaluation is complete.

Thank you very much for your time and for making this work available to the community.
